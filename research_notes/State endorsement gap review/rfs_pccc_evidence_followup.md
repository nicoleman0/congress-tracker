# PCCC evidence classification follow-up

Checked 8 November 2026. This is the final focused source pass, not a new roster collection.

## What can verify an endorsement?

### Takeaway

Issuer-domain hosting is not required. Reliable primary reporting that records an organization's direct statement can verify an endorsement. The earlier notes' issuer-domain-only restriction was an audit interpretation, not an agreed user rule, and has been removed.

### Cited findings

- David Wildstein's June 5, 2025 New Jersey Globe report says PCCC supports Steve Fulop or Ras Baraka in the Democratic gubernatorial primary. It directly quotes PCCC cofounder Adam Green explaining the choice and reports Green discussing a membership survey. This is primary reporting of the organization's decision, rather than an unsupported candidate claim. [Original report](https://newjerseyglobe.com/governor/national-progressive-group-backs-fulop-baraka/)
- Dana DiFilippo's June 6 New Jersey Monitor report independently lists PCCC among both candidates' endorsements. [Report](https://newjerseymonitor.com/2025/06/06/eleven-candidates-are-vying-to-succeed-gov-murphy-who-is-endorsing-them/)

### Inferences

- Classify the 2025 NJ primary coendorsement as verified. Its missing issuer archive is a provenance/completeness gap, not an unverified endorsement event.
- New Jersey is now the eighth PCCC state with a verified in-scope historical endorsement source in this audit. The other seven positive states concern 2016/2018 only. These endorsements do not establish election outcomes or current service.

### Gaps

- Neither news report supplies a complete PCCC state-office slate for 2024–2026. No winner or current-term qualification is inferred.

## What does the Mohajer announcement establish?

### Takeaway

The February 12, 2024 Mohajer announcement documents a named PCCC spokesperson statement. It is stronger than a logo or a bare candidate assertion, but this focused search did not recover independent reporting of that particular endorsement.

### Cited findings

- Mohajer's campaign page explicitly announces a California Senate District 37 endorsement and quotes Hannah Riddle as PCCC Candidate Services Director. The host is the candidate's campaign; it should not be mislabeled as an issuer-hosted original or independent reporting. [Campaign announcement](https://alexmohajer.com/progressive-change-campaign-committee-endorses-alex-mohajer-for-california-state-senate-district-37/)
- ABC News reporter Brittany Shepherd directly quoted Riddle and identified her candidate-services role in June 2023. [ABC report](https://abcnews.com/Politics/progressives-launch-campaign-flip-school-board-seats-nationwide/story?id=100246945)
- AJC reporter Martha Dalton identified the same role and quoted Riddle explaining PCCC's endorsement of Laura Judge in February 2024. That school-board endorsement is outside this feature's office scope. [AJC report](https://www.ajc.com/education/georgia-school-board-races-attract-more-local-and-national-interest/6KJL7UM4GRFYDEXV46PGKMPT2I/)

### Inferences

- Riddle's organizational role is independently corroborated. That supports the announcement's attribution, but does not independently authenticate every quoted statement on the campaign page.
- Preserve this as a documented named-spokesperson announcement with event corroboration pending. Do not reject it solely for being off the issuer's domain. A reviewer can assess the specific statement's authenticity without changing the organization's eligibility rule.

### Gaps

- No independent Mohajer endorsement report or original PCCC announcement was recovered in this focused pass. No new 2024–2026 legislative winner/current-term qualification was established.

## What remains blocked?

### Takeaway

The remaining PCCC problems are incomplete slate discovery and unproved champions-list meaning, not a ban on independent journalism.

### Cited findings

- PCCC describes endorsements and other support separately. Its services page does not define every historical champions entry as endorsed. [PCCC services](https://www.boldprogressives.org/resources-for-candidates/)

### Inferences

- Accept verified individual events from suitable primary evidence, including reporter-recorded organizational statements. Keep originating statement, host, publication date and corroboration distinct.
- Do not promote an entire champions list because one entry is independently confirmed.

### Gaps

- No complete later-cycle state-office list or blanket champions definition was recovered. The updated source ledger, coverage table and gap register preserve these limits. No implementation was started.
