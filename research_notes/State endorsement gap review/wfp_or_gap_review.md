# WFP and Our Revolution historical gap review

## Which state and chapter sources were actually reviewed?

### Takeaway
All 18 WFP state organizations linked in the national navigation were inspected. The official state sitemap exposed a nineteenth state, West Virginia, and its page was also retrieved. This closes the missing state-page review, not the all-local-chapter or current-term audit. Checked 8 November 2026.

### Cited findings
- WFP navigation and the official state sitemap differ: the sitemap also lists West Virginia. The WV page links its April 2022 endorsement release, which names a state Senate candidate and mixes state and local races. Its quoted total must not be treated as a legislator count. [State sitemap](https://workingfamilies.org/state-sitemap.xml), [WV page](https://workingfamilies.org/state/west-virginia/), [2022 release](https://workingfamilies.org/2022/04/west-virginia-wfp-announces-59-new-endorsements/).
- California and Wisconsin www-host state requests initially failed with SSL errors; the normal bare-host page URLs retrieved successfully. Every one of the 19 state organizations therefore has a successful landing-page inspection. Their news panels are selected recent links, not complete historical indexes. [California](https://workingfamilies.org/state/california/), [Wisconsin](https://workingfamilies.org/state/wisconsin/).
- The public WFP post sitemap exposed 471 post URLs. Forty-one URLs matching historical office/endorsement/guide discovery terms were recorded, without pretending that each was an in-scope source or had been read. The site's news archive separately exposes pagination through page 54. [Post sitemap](https://workingfamilies.org/post-sitemap.xml), [News archive](https://workingfamilies.org/news/).
- National Our Revolution's 2023 post explicitly refers to “Our Revolution Massachusetts organizers” and names a MetroWest leader. This supplies parent recognition missing in the first audit, with historical scope. It does not certify a complete current affiliate census. [Parent recognition](https://ourrevolution.com/medicare-for-all-in-massachusetts/).
- ORMA's current About page says it has 13 local affiliates but does not supply a complete linked roster on the inspected page. An August 2024 newsletter names Arlington, Cambridge, Concord and MetroWest as affiliates. This is a historical state-parent trail for those four bodies. Its particular endorsement is for Governor's Council, which remains out of scope. [About](https://ourrevolutionma.com/who-we-are/), [2024 affiliate naming](https://ourrevolutionma.com/2024-08-06-organizing-notes-38-a-d-for-the-mass-state-legislature-endorsements-actions-and-a-little-good-news/).
- National 2023 guide/recap pages recognize Greater Worcester, Ohio and Twin Cities groups. They provide dated parent recognition, not a current directory or complete independent legislative archives. [Guide](https://ourrevolution.com/2023-general-election-guide/), [Recap](https://ourrevolution.com/2023-general-election-recap/).
- The Wisconsin affiliate's own site calls itself a national affiliate and has year-labelled endorsement sections spanning 2022–2026. One card under 2024 was visually checked: “WE ENDORSE RYAN CLANCY”, Wisconsin State Assembly District 19. The other cards were not individually read. Parent confirmation is still missing. [Endorsement archive](https://www.ourwisconsinrev.com/endorsements), [inspected original image](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/d6821e_83a05f5074b041f4b77863e7e2303945~mv2.png).

### Inferences
- WFP's official sitemap is a better historical discovery path than relying on the latest eight news links per state.
- Our Revolution's official chapter census remains the larger blocker. Historical recognition and current self-description should remain separate evidence fields.

### Gaps
- Maryland chapter root returned HTTP 403 in direct retrieval and was unavailable to the web reader. Search snippets and third-party lists were not promoted to verified chapter endorsements.
- `ourrevolution.com/groups/` remains 404. The current page sitemap/public API exposes 12 pages and no replacement directory. The mission page had no usable directory content; the press archive did not restore the old census.
- Internet Archive availability requests for the original groups and endorsements URLs, a direct archived groups URL and the web reader alternative all failed access checks/TLS retrieval. No archival capture date or archived affiliation was invented.
- The partial chapter review has 29 rows: 19 WFP state organizations and 10 Our Revolution/state-local bodies with their actual recognition or blocked status. It is not a nationwide chapter count.

## What remains before implementation?

### Takeaway
The source inventory improved, but the nationwide eligibility gate is still open. The next research should join official term-winning elections to these dated sources and close the remaining affiliate census.

### Cited findings
- The comparison matrix now has 24 WFP source-positive states, up from 22 through NJ and WV, and18 confirmed Our Revolution source-positive states, unchanged. Wisconsin adds one conditional affiliate lead, kept not_established because national affiliation is unresolved. The union is 30 states with some in-scope source evidence, not 30 complete rosters. Per-source evidence and limitations are in `wfp_or_coverage.tsv`, `wfp_or_sources.tsv` and the separate governor table.

### Inferences
- Use explicit year/stage/issuer evidence for the term-winning election. Join official service and term dates before presenting an eligible current-officeholder count.
- For each remaining state, resolve the applicable chapter/source inventory or document a concrete missing source. Absence from either national page remains unknown, not zero officials.
- Image archives need transcription and review; mutable lists need dated snapshots. Do not substitute a current list for an earlier endorsement cycle.
